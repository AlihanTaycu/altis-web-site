# Altis Corporate Presentation Design

Date: 2026-06-15
Language: Turkish first, English localization later
Output target: Editable Canva presentation

## Goal

Create a new corporate overview presentation for Altis Teknoloji that can be used with new customers, solution partners, and general corporate audiences. The deck should communicate who Altis is, what technologies it works with, why it is credible, and how it delivers projects end to end.

The old PDF presentation at `C:\Users\aliha\Downloads\Altis Teknoloji Kurumsal Sunum (1).pdf` will be used as an information source, especially for company history. The new website and exported Markdown content will be used as the current messaging and brand source.

## Visual Direction

Use the approved "Quiet Precision" direction:

- Apple-like minimalism.
- White-first, spacious layouts.
- Large, confident headlines.
- Short body copy.
- Very restrained use of lines and cyan accents.
- No dense cards, heavy dashboards, decorative hero graphics, or repeated eyebrow labels.
- Avoid visible UI-like blocks unless they clarify a specific concept.
- Use Altis navy `#00125C` as the primary brand color.
- Use Altis cyan `#18B7D6` only as a small accent.
- Use soft backgrounds such as `#FFFFFF` and `#F6F8FC`.
- Use an Inter-like sans-serif in Canva.

## Content Rules

- Do not use the term "Auto-ID".
- Use "RFID" as the primary technology term.
- Other allowed technology terms: IoT, IIoT, RTLS, endustriyel yazilim, saha entegrasyonu, veri toplama, operasyon gorunurlugu.
- Product families should be summarized, not presented as a catalog.
- The presentation should feel like a corporate capability story, not a product brochure.
- History should be expanded beyond a single slide.

## Deck Scope

Target length: 20-24 slides.

The deck should work both in a live meeting and as a PDF/shareable corporate profile after the meeting. Because it may be sent to new partners or customers, each slide should be understandable without long speaker explanation, but still remain visually minimal.
